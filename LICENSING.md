## Third-party material

Dependencies, fonts and other third-party material keep their own
licenses and copyright notices. `LICENSES/` holds the notices this
repository retains: `LICENSES/fonts/` for the fonts used by the site and
the local interface, and `LICENSES/crates/` for crates whose published
archives omit their license files. Release archives also carry
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/`, collected from the
locked dependency graph. Do not remove an attribution or present upstream
material as this project's own.

## Legal source fixtures

The files under `sources/` directories in `examples/` are fixtures written
for this project: summaries of, and excerpts from, statutes, rules and
other legal materials. Each is labeled as a fixture or as not an official
edition. Where a fixture quotes official legal text, the project claims no
rights in that text.
