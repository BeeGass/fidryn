Texas Constitution article XVI § 50 and Texas Property Code § 41.002
(fixture excerpt).
The homestead of a family or of a single adult person is protected from
forced sale except for debts enumerated in article XVI § 50. If used for
the purposes of an urban home, the homestead consists of not more than
10 acres of land, which may be in one or more lots, together with any
improvements thereon. Whether a place has homestead character is not a
Boolean. This is a research fixture. Not official code. Not legal advice.
