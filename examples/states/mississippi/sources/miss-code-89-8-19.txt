Mississippi Code Annotated section 89-8-19 (fixture excerpt).
Residential Landlord and Tenant Act. Length of term of tenancy; notice to
terminate tenancy. Laws 1991, ch. 478, section 10, effective July 1, 1991;
Laws 2022, ch. 501, section 11.

(1) Unless the rental agreement fixes a definite term a tenancy shall be
week to week in case of a tenant who pays weekly rent, and in all other
cases month to month.

(2) The landlord or the tenant may terminate a week-to-week tenancy by
written notice given to the other at least seven (7) days prior to the
termination date.

(3) The landlord or the tenant may terminate a month-to-month tenancy by a
written notice given to the other at least thirty (30) days prior to the
termination date.

(4) Notwithstanding the provisions of this section or any other provision of
this chapter to the contrary, notice to terminate a tenancy shall not be
required to be given when the landlord or tenant has committed a substantial
violation of the rental agreement or this chapter that materially affects
health or safety.

Whether a substantial violation materially affecting health or safety
occurred, and whether a tenancy is week to week or month to month when the
agreement is silent, remain determinations. Not an official edition.
