New Jersey Statutes Annotated 46:8-21.1 (fixture excerpt).
2025 N.J. Rev. Stat. § 46:8-21.1; N.J. Legislature compilation through
P.L.2025, c.405. Last amended L.2010, c.34, s.11.

Within 30 days after the termination of the tenant's lease or licensee's
agreement, the owner or lessee shall return by personal delivery,
registered or certified mail the sum so deposited plus the tenant's
portion of the interest or earnings accumulated thereon, less any
charges expended in accordance with the terms of a contract, lease, or
agreement, to the tenant or licensee. The interest or earnings and any
such deductions shall be itemized and the tenant notified thereof by
personal delivery, registered or certified mail. No deductions shall be
made from a security deposit of a tenant who remains in possession of
the rental premises.

Within five business days after the tenant is caused to be displaced by
fire, flood, condemnation, or evacuation, and an authorized public
official posts the premises or a building inspector certifies that
displacement is expected to continue longer than seven days, the owner
or lessee shall have available and return the deposit plus interest,
less lawful charges and rent due, upon demand.

Within 15 business days after a lease terminates under the Safe Housing
Act, P.L.2008, c.111 (C.46:8-9.6), the owner or lessee shall have
available and return the security upon demand, with an itemized
statement.

In any action by a tenant for the return of moneys due under this
section, the court upon finding for the tenant shall award recovery of
double the amount of said moneys, together with full costs of any
action and, in the court's discretion, reasonable attorney's fees.

Whether a sum was wrongfully withheld is a finding for the court. The
doubling follows that finding; it is not a separate Boolean. See Hale
v. Farrakhan, 390 N.J. Super. 335 (App. Div. 2007); Kang In Yi v.
Re/Max Fortune Props., Inc., 338 N.J. Super. 534 (App. Div. 2001).
