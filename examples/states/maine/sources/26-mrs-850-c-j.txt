Maine Revised Statutes Title 26, sections 850-C and 850-J (fixture excerpt).
Labor and Industry, Employment Practices, Subchapter 6-C, Paid Family and
Medical Leave.

Section 850-C. Medical leave benefits are not payable during the first 7
calendar days of the leave, except that an employee may use accrued sick
or vacation pay or other paid leave provided under a collective bargaining
agreement or employer policy during those first 7 calendar days.

The weekly benefit amount is calculated as follows: the portion of the
covered individual's average weekly wage that is equal to or less than 50%
of the state average weekly wage must be replaced at a rate of 90%; and
the portion that is more than 50% of the state average weekly wage must be
replaced at a rate of 66% up to the maximum weekly benefit. The maximum
weekly benefit amount is the state average weekly wage.

Section 850-J. Except for an employee who has not been employed for at
least 120 days, an employee who exercises the right to family leave or
medical leave under this subchapter is entitled, upon return from that
leave, to be restored by the employer to the position held when the leave
commenced or to an equivalent position with equivalent employment
benefits, pay and other terms and conditions of employment.

An employer may not discharge, fire, suspend, expel or discipline,
through the application of attendance policies or otherwise, or threaten
or in any manner discriminate against an employee for the exercise of any
right to which the employee is entitled under this subchapter.

Whether a health condition, bonding event, or safe-leave event qualifies
remains a determination. Not an official edition.
