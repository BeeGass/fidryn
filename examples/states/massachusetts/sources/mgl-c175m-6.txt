Massachusetts General Laws chapter 175M, section 6
(fixture excerpt). Contributions.

(a) For each employee or covered contract worker, an employer or
a covered business entity shall remit to the Family and Employment
Security Trust Fund contributions in the form and manner as
determined by the department. The family leave and medical leave
contribution rates set forth in this section shall be adjusted
annually as specified in subsection (e) of section 7.

(c)(1) For medical leave, an employer shall not deduct more than
40 per cent of the contribution required for an employee by
subsection (a) from that employee's wages and shall remit the full
contribution required under said subsection (a) to the trust fund.

(c)(2) For family leave, an employer may deduct not more than
100 per cent of the contribution required for an employee by
subsection (a) from that employee's wages, and shall remit the
full contribution required under subsection (a) to the trust fund.

(d) Notwithstanding subsection (c), an employer employing less
than 25 employees in the commonwealth shall not be required to pay
the employer portion of premiums for family and medical leave;
provided, however, that such employer shall remit, for each
employee, 100 per cent of the family leave contribution and
40 per cent of the medical leave contribution as otherwise
required under subsection (a).

(f) Contributions shall not be required for wages above the
contribution and benefit base limit established annually by the
federal Social Security Administration pursuant to 42 U.S.C. 430.
For calendar year 2026 that wage base is $184,500.

2026 Department of Family and Medical Leave published rates
(calendar year 2026; same as 2025):

Employers with 25 or more covered individuals remit 0.88 percent
of eligible wages, of which 0.70 percent is medical leave and
0.18 percent is family leave. Up to 40 percent of the medical
leave contribution (0.28 percent of eligible wages) and up to
100 percent of the family leave contribution (0.18 percent of
eligible wages) may be withheld from the covered individual.
The employer remits the remaining 60 percent of medical leave
(0.42 percent of eligible wages).

Employers with fewer than 25 covered individuals remit 0.46 percent
of eligible wages (0.28 percent medical employee share plus
0.18 percent family), with no required employer portion.

Chapter 101 of the Acts of 2026 shifts the employer contribution
from medical leave toward family leave effective January 1, 2027
and is outside this 2026 snapshot.
