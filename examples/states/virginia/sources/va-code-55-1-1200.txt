Virginia Code § 55.1-1200. Definitions. (fixture excerpt).
LIS Code of Virginia, Title 55.1, Chapter 12, retrieved 2026-09-17.

"Security deposit" means any refundable deposit of money that is
furnished by a tenant to a landlord to secure the performance of the
terms and conditions of a rental agreement, as a security for damages
to the leased premises, or as a pet deposit. However, such money shall
be deemed an application deposit until the commencement date of the
rental agreement. "Security deposit" does not include a damage
insurance policy or renter's insurance policy, as those terms are
defined in § 55.1-1206, purchased by a landlord to provide coverage
for a tenant.

"Damage insurance" means a bond or commercial insurance coverage as
specified in the rental agreement to secure the performance by the
tenant of the terms and conditions of the rental agreement and to
replace all or part of a security deposit.

"Good faith" means honesty in fact in the conduct of the transaction
concerned.

"Rent" means all money, other than a security deposit, owed or paid
to the landlord under the rental agreement, including prepaid rent
paid more than one month in advance of the rent due date.

A refundable pet deposit is a security deposit and counts toward the
two-month cap in § 55.1-1226. A nonrefundable pet fee is not a
security deposit under this definition.
