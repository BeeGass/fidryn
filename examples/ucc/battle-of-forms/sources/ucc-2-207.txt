U.C.C. § 2-207 (fixture excerpt).
A definite and seasonable expression of acceptance or a written
confirmation operates as an acceptance even though it states terms
additional to or different from those offered or agreed upon. Between
merchants, additional terms become part of the contract unless the offer
expressly limits acceptance to the terms of the offer, they materially
alter it, or notification of objection has been given or is given within
a reasonable time. Material alteration is not encoded as a Boolean.
