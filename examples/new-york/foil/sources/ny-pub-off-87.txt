New York Public Officers Law § 87 (fixture excerpt), with the five

Each agency shall, in accordance with its published rules, make available
for public inspection and copying all records except those records or
portions thereof that may be withheld under § 87(2), including records
that if disclosed would interfere with law-enforcement investigations.
Within five business days of receipt of a written request, the agency
shall grant or deny access or acknowledge the request and state an
approximate date. The law-enforcement exemption is not a Boolean. This
