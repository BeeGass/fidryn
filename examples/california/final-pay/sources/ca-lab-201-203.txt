California Labor Code §§ 201-203 (fixture excerpt).
If an employer discharges an employee, wages earned and unpaid at the
time of discharge are due and payable immediately. A quit is not encoded
here as the next regular payday; section 202 uses a 72-hour rule that
this slice leaves outside_scope. Section 203 waiting-time penalties turn
on willful failure and are not a Boolean. This is a research fixture.
