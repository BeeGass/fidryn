California Civil Code § 1793.22 (fixture excerpt).
A reasonable number of repair attempts is presumed if the same
nonconformity has been subject to repair four or more times, or the
vehicle is out of service by reason of repair of nonconformities for a
cumulative total of more than 30 calendar days. Substantial impairment
of use, value, or safety is not a Boolean. This is a research fixture.
