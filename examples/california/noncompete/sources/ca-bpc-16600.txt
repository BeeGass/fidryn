California Business and Professions Code § 16600 (fixture excerpt).
Except as provided in this chapter, every contract by which anyone is
restrained from engaging in a lawful profession, trade, or business of
any kind is to that extent void. Section 16601 may permit a restraint
ancillary to a sale of the goodwill of a business; that exception is
not a Boolean. This is a research fixture. Not official code. Not legal
advice.
