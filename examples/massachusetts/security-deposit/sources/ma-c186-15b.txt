Massachusetts General Laws chapter 186 § 15B (fixture excerpt).
The lessor shall, within thirty days after the termination of occupancy
under a tenancy-at-will or the end of the tenancy as specified in a
lease, return the security deposit or any balance due, together with an
itemized list of any damages. A tenant may recover three times the
amount of the security deposit or any balance due, plus interest and
reasonable attorney's fees, for listed violations; that multiplier is
not a Boolean. This is a research fixture. Not official code. Not legal
advice.
