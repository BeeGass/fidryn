Fair Labor Standards Act, 29 U.S.C. § 206, with the savings clause in
29 U.S.C. § 218 (fixture excerpt).
Every employer shall pay to each of his employees who in any workweek
is engaged in commerce or in the production of goods for commerce, or
is employed in an enterprise engaged in commerce or in the production
of goods for commerce, wages at the federal floor of $7.25 an hour.
No provision of this chapter excuses noncompliance with any Federal or
State law or municipal ordinance establishing a minimum wage higher
than the federal floor; the higher wage controls as to the employee.
