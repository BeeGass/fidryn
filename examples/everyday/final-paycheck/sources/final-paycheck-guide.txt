Final paycheck timing (fixture guide). Points at California Labor Code
§§ 201-203 and the default next-payday rule used in many jurisdictions.
Where no more specific state rule is selected, earned wages are due by
the next regular payday after the employment ends. California Labor
Code § 201 requires that wages earned and unpaid at discharge are due
and payable immediately; waiting-time penalties under § 203 are not
encoded here. Whether the California immediate-pay rule applies to a
worker is not a Boolean. This is a research fixture. Not official code.
Not legal advice.
