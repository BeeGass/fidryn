26 U.S.C. § 83(b) (fixture excerpt).
If property is transferred in connection with the performance of services,
the person performing such services may elect to include in gross income
the excess of fair market value over the amount paid. The election must
be made not later than 30 days after the date of the transfer. Whether
property is subject to a substantial risk of forfeiture is not a Boolean.
