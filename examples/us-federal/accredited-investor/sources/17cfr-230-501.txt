17 C.F.R. § 230.501 (fixture excerpt).
Accredited investor includes a natural person whose individual income
exceeded $200,000 in each of the two most recent years, or joint income
with that person's spouse or spousal equivalent exceeded $300,000, with
a reasonable expectation of the same income level in the current year;
and a natural person whose net worth exceeds $1,000,000, excluding the
value of the primary residence. Sophistication and other categories are
not encoded as Booleans.
