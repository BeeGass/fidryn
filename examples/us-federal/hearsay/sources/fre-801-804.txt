Fed. R. Evid. 801-804 (fixture excerpt).
Hearsay is a statement that the declarant does not make while testifying
at the current trial or hearing and that a party offers in evidence to
prove the truth of the matter asserted. Rule 803(6) excepts records of a
regularly conducted activity if the record was made at or near the time
by someone with knowledge, kept in the course of a regularly conducted
activity, making the record was a regular practice, and the opponent does
not show that the source or method of preparation indicates a lack of
trustworthiness. Those findings are not Booleans.
