§ 2 — What it is

Instruments, not slogans

Bounded slices of trusts, tax, and statute-shaped rules as checkable programs, with outside_scope named so omissions stay visible.

Honest outcomes

Determinate only when invariant across every still-admissible resolution, or when a competent authority has already decided. Otherwise suspended, contingent, or another named kind.

A local mill

fidryn ui binds 127.0.0.1 only. It checks, runs, explores, and renders modules. It never files.

§ 3 — Six honest outcomes

A query answers with exactly one of six kinds, and every answer carries the declared modelBoundary. Each kind answers one question.

  1. Is there one answer, invariant across every admissible completion, or already determined by a competent authority?

    Determinate

  2. Do still-admissible answers disagree?

    Contingent

  3. Does evaluation need a legal operation the case does not discharge, with no covering certificate?

    Suspended

  4. Do staged effects disagree with no single applicable doctrine?

    NormConflict

  5. Does the case ask for something outside the declared model or the module’s competence?

    OutsideCompetence

  6. Can the admitted model not be satisfied?

    Inconsistent
